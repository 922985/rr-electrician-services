ELECTRICAL SAFETY FIRST

Safety is the most important part of every electrical job. Use proper protective equipment and follow safe working practices.

Professional Safety Equipment

Safety Helmet

Use a suitable protective helmet where there is a risk of head injury.

Protective Gloves

Use correctly rated electrical insulating gloves when the task requires them.

Electrical Safety Tools

Use properly insulated and inspected tools suitable for the job.

Safe Work Practices

Keep the work area organized and follow safe electrical procedures.

Important Electrical Safety Rules

Switch Off Power

Isolate the electrical supply before working. Prevent accidental reconnection and verify that the circuit is de-energized using suitable equipment.

Use Proper Equipment

Use tools and protective equipment rated for the electrical work being performed.

Keep Work Area Safe

Keep the working area dry, clean and free from unnecessary obstacles.

Check Wiring

Inspect wiring, connections and protective devices for damage or faults.
